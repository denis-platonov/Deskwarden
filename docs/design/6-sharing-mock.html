TURN 6 · PERSONAL SHARING

Share a record with a person, not a link

You type someone's email, and the record shows up in their vault, like Keeper does. They can see, copy and autofill it, but not edit or delete it, and it changes when you edit it. Under the hood this is a hidden Bitwarden organisation. No organisation, collection or organisation name appears anywhere below, and the rail's ORGANISATIONS section leaves it out. A Send is a link (square tile, paper plane, round-ended state pills). A share is a person (round avatar, person mark, square-cornered share pills). Mock only. Nothing is wired up, and the data is made up.
